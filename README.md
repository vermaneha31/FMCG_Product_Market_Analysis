# FMCG Product & Market Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes FMCG product and market performance using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.

The main objective is to compare our product performance with the overall market and identify products that require further investigation.

## 🎯 Objective

The project focuses on understanding:

- Our sales vs market sales
- Product-wise market share
- Our price vs competitor price
- Customer ratings
- Customer complaints
- Region-wise sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Product performance ranking
- Sales gap between our sales and market sales
- Business insights and recommendations

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Our Sales vs Market Sales
Compared our product sales with total market sales to identify performance gaps.

### 2. Market Share Analysis
Calculated the percentage of market sales captured by our products.

### 3. Price Comparison
Compared our product prices with competitor prices.

### 4. Customer Rating Analysis
Analyzed customer ratings to identify highly rated and low-rated products.

### 5. Customer Complaint Analysis
Reviewed complaint categories to identify possible customer concerns.

### 6. Region-wise Sales Analysis
Grouped sales by region to understand regional performance.

### 7. Monthly Sales Trend
Analyzed monthly sales to identify overall sales trends.

### 8. Product Performance Ranking
Ranked products based on our sales performance.

### 9. Sales Gap Analysis
Calculated the difference between market sales and our sales.

### 10. Business Insights & Recommendations
Identified important business findings and suggested areas for further investigation.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Soap is the highest-selling product with **10,500 units**.
- Body Lotion has the lowest sales with **4,800 units**.
- Shampoo has the highest sales gap of **3,800 units**.
- Soap has the highest customer rating of **4.4/5**.
- Shampoo has the highest price at **Rs. 299**.
- Our overall market share in the selected dataset is **67.75%**.
- Our prices are higher than competitor prices across all five products.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Review Shampoo's pricing and competitive positioning.
- Investigate the reasons behind Body Lotion's lower sales and rating.
- Study the factors contributing to Soap's strong performance.
- Monitor competitor pricing regularly.
- Collect more detailed customer complaint data for deeper analysis.

This project demonstrates how Python and Pandas can be used to analyze business data, identify product performance gaps, compare competitors, and generate actionable business insights.
